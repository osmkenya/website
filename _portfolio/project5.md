In March and April 2025, OSM Kenya members supported the Humanitarian OpenStreetMap Team – Open Mapping Hub Eastern and Southern Africa on an innovative project: Photomapping for Sustainable Mobility using Street-Level Imagery (SLI) in Nakuru, Kenya.

This project aimed to improve the detail, accuracy, and usability of OpenStreetMap (OSM) data by leveraging the power of street-level imagery and AI-assisted mapping tools. By leveraging high-resolution imagery captured along Nakuru's streets, the team transformed photos into valuable geospatial data that directly supports sustainable mobility initiatives.
