Every year, **World Cleanup Day** brings together millions of people around the globe with one mission: to create a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable planet. In 2025, OSM Kenya proudly joined this global movement by organizing a cleanup activity in Kibera, Nairobi. 

OSM Kenya volunteers, together with community members and local partners, Kibera Eco Care collected litter and plastic from identified hotspots in Kibera and sorted the waste for recycling and proper disposal. Additionally, we mapped cleanup points to support future waste management initiatives and inform local planners.
