# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: December 2025**

---

## About This Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how OpenStreetMap Kenya collects, uses, and shares information when you visit {{ site.title }} (the "Site").

---

## Information We Collect

### 1. Registration Form Data

When you complete our membership registration form (powered by Google Forms), we collect:

- **Personal Information:** Name, email address, location
- **Optional Information:** Phone number, mapping experience, areas of interest
- **Technical Information:** Timestamp of submission

**Purpose:** To process membership applications and communicate with members about community activities.

**Retention:** We retain registration data for the duration of your membership and up to 2 years after membership ends.

### 2. Website Usage Information

{% if site.analytics.google %}

**Google Analytics** (if enabled):

When you visit the Site, we automatically collect:
- IP address (anonymized)
- Browser type and version
- Device information
- Pages visited and time spent
- Referring website

We use cookies for this purpose. "Cookies" are small data files stored on your device.

**Purpose:** To understand how visitors use our site and improve user experience.

**Opt-out:** You can opt-out of Google Analytics here: <https://tools.google.com/dlpage/gaoptout>

For more about Google's privacy practices: <https://www.google.com/intl/en/policies/privacy/>

{% else %}

We do **not** use Google Analytics or tracking cookies. However, basic server logs may be collected by our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) for security and performance purposes.

{% endif %}

### 3. GitHub Pages Hosting

Our website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may collect:
- Technical logs (IP addresses, browser information)
- Access times and requested pages

**Purpose:** Site hosting, security, and performance.

For GitHub's privacy practices: <https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-privacy-statement>

---

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

1. **Process membership applications** and maintain our member database
2. **Communicate with members** about mapping events, training, and community activities
3. **Improve our website** and understand how visitors interact with our content
4. **Comply with legal obligations** and protect our rights

---

## Information Sharing

We do **not** sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties.

We may share information with:

- **OpenStreetMap Foundation:** For coordination of regional activities (only with your consent)
- **Event Partners:** When you register for specific events or workshops
- **Legal Requirements:** If required by law or to protect our legal rights

---

## Third-Party Services

Our website uses the following third-party services:

- **Google Forms:** Registration form hosting
- **GitHub Pages:** Website hosting
- **Google Fonts:** Website typography (no tracking)

Each service has its own privacy policy governing their data practices.

## Your Rights

You have the right to:

- **Access** your personal information we hold
- **Correct** inaccurate information
- **Delete** your information (right to be forgotten)
- **Withdraw consent** for data processing
- **Opt-out** of communications

**To exercise these rights**, contact us at <a href="mailto:{{ site.email }}">{{ site.email }}</a>.

---

## Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

---

## Children's Privacy

Our website is not directed to individuals under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe we have collected information from a child, please contact us immediately.

---

## International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries outside Kenya, including the United States (where GitHub and Google services are based). We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place for such transfers.

## Cookies Policy

### What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit websites.

### Cookies We Use

{% if site.analytics.google %}
- **Analytics Cookies** (Google Analytics): Track website usage
- **Session Cookies:** Maintain site functionality
{% else %}
We do not use cookies on this website.
{% endif %}

### Managing Cookies

You can control cookies through your browser settings:
- [Chrome](https://support.google.com/chrome/answer/95647)
- [Firefox](https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/cookies-information-websites-store-on-your-computer)
- [Safari](https://support.apple.com/guide/safari/manage-cookies-and-website-data-sfri11471/mac)

More information: <http://www.allaboutcookies.org>
